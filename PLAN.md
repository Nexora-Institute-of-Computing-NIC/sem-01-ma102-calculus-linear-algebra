# MA102 — Calculus & Linear Algebra — PLAN

**Semester:** 1 · **Credits:** 4 · **Level:** Beginner
**Theory:** 45 hrs · **Tutorial:** 15 hrs · **Tools:** Python (NumPy, SymPy, matplotlib)

## Syllabus

### Unit 1 — Limits and Continuity (8 lectures)
1. Functions of one variable — review
2. Intuitive idea of a limit
3. Formal definition of a limit (epsilon-delta)
4. One-sided limits
5. Continuity and discontinuities
6. Intermediate value theorem
7. Limits at infinity and asymptotes
8. The squeeze theorem

### Unit 2 — Differentiation (10 lectures)
1. Definition of the derivative
2. Rules — sum, product, quotient
3. Chain rule
4. Implicit differentiation
5. Higher-order derivatives
6. Taylor and Maclaurin series (introduction)
7. Maxima and minima
8. Convexity and inflection points
9. Applications — related rates
10. Applications — optimisation problems

### Unit 3 — Integration (9 lectures)
1. Antiderivatives
2. Riemann sums and the definite integral
3. Fundamental theorem of calculus
4. Substitution method
5. Integration by parts
6. Improper integrals
7. Areas, volumes, arc length
8. Numerical integration — trapezoidal, Simpson
9. Applications in probability (continuous distributions)

### Unit 4 — Linear Algebra: Vectors and Matrices (9 lectures)
1. Vectors in R^n — algebra and geometry
2. Dot product, norms, distances
3. Matrices — notation, addition, scalar multiplication
4. Matrix multiplication
5. Transpose and inverse (2x2 and general)
6. Determinant — definition and properties
7. Systems of linear equations
8. Gaussian elimination
9. LU decomposition

### Unit 5 — Vector Spaces and Eigenvalues (9 lectures)
1. Vector spaces — axioms and examples
2. Subspaces, span, linear independence
3. Basis and dimension
4. Rank and nullity
5. Linear transformations and matrices
6. Eigenvalues and eigenvectors — definition
7. Computing eigenvalues and eigenvectors
8. Diagonalisation
9. Applications — PCA, Markov chains, graph centrality

## Practical Labs (15)

1. Plotting functions and visualising limits
2. Numerical differentiation and comparison to analytic
3. Finding roots with Newton's method
4. Taylor series approximation of sin, cos, exp
5. Optimisation with scipy.optimize
6. Numerical integration — trapezoidal and Simpson rules
7. Curve fitting — least squares with NumPy
8. Vector operations and geometry with NumPy
9. Matrix operations and determinant with NumPy
10. Solving linear systems — direct and iterative
11. Gaussian elimination implementation
12. Eigenvalues and eigenvectors with NumPy
13. Diagonalisation examples
14. PCA on a small dataset from scratch
15. PageRank — a Markov-chain application
